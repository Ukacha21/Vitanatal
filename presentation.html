Root Cause Breakdown

Why Do Avoidable C-Sections Happen?

Avoidable surgical deliveries are driven by information asymmetry and late clinical reaction.

⚠️ 1. Late Breech Detection (Weeks 37–39)
Breech position is typically confirmed at the last ultrasound. With low amniotic fluid remaining, there is no time for natural exercises, leading to an automatic planned surgical booking.
⚠️ 2. The "Admission Cascade" (False Labor)
Anxious mothers enter the ward during Braxton Hicks. Clinicians administer synthetic oxytocin (Pitocin); if the cervix isn't effaced, labor stalls, triggering an "emergency" C-section.
⚠️ 3. Occiput Posterior ("Sunny-Side Up") Dystocia
The fetal occiput presses against the maternal sacrum, creating severe prolonged back labor, maternal exhaustion, and surgical vacuum/C-section extraction.
⚠️ 4. Maternal Anxiety & Episodic Blind Spots
Expectant mothers spend 4 to 6 weeks between clinic visits without reassurance, driving anxiety-driven requests for elective surgical dates out of fear.

Proprietary TinyML

Algorithm I: Spatial Heart-Kick Vectoring

Deriving continuous fetal orientation without ultrasound beam steering.

📐 Mathematical Derivation

Acoustic Heart Centroid ($\vec{C}_{\text{heart}}$):

C_heart = Σ(A_i · P_i) / Σ(A_i)

Kinetic Kick Centroid ($\vec{C}_{\text{kick}}$):

C_kick = Σ(Δa_k · S_k) / Σ(Δa_k)

Fetal Presentation Vector:

V_presentation = C_kick - C_heart
🧭 Classification Output Mapping
Cephalic (Vertex) — V_y > 0
Kicks in fundus, heart near pelvis. Ideal for vaginal birth.
Breech Presentation — V_y < 0
Heart superior, kicks inferior. High surgical risk if uncorrected.
Transverse Lie — |V_x| ≫ |V_y|
Baby lying sideways across pelvic axis. Requires OB intervention.
Labor Electrophysiology

Algorithm II: Uterine EHG Labor Triage

Stopping the premature hospital admission cascade before synthetic oxytocin is administered.

⚡ Braxton Hicks (False Labor)
• Peak Frequency ($F_{\text{peak}}$): < 0.30 Hz
• Wave Propagation Velocity: < 1.8 cm/s (Isolated local spasms)
• High/Low Energy Ratio ($R_{\text{H/L}}$): < 0.4 (Low electrical power)
• System Action: Advises mother to rest and hydrate at home.
🔥 Active True Labor Progression
• Peak Frequency ($F_{\text{peak}}$): > 0.35 Hz – 0.85 Hz
• Wave Propagation Velocity: 2.5 – 6.0 cm/s (Organ-wide synchronized wave)
• High/Low Energy Ratio ($R_{\text{H/L}}$): > 1.2 (Active cervical effacement)
• System Action: Notifies hospital & instructs parents to depart.
Clinical Impact: Prevents premature cervical ripening failures, reduces synthetic oxytocin hyperstimulation, and directly prevents emergency cesareans.
